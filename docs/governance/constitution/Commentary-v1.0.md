# Commentary v1.0

## Purpose

This Commentary accompanies the AgentiCubed Constitution but is not part of the Constitution itself.

It explains the intent behind constitutional provisions without creating, modifying, expanding, or restricting constitutional authority.

Where Commentary and the Constitution differ, the Constitution controls.

---

## Design Principles

The Constitution intentionally governs enduring principles rather than implementation details.

It defines authority rather than workflow.

It defines accountability rather than process.

It defines trust relationships rather than technology.

Accordingly, implementation choices—including programming languages, repositories, frameworks, orchestration systems, deployment models, storage systems, and operational tooling—belong in subordinate governance documents rather than the Constitution.

---

## Constitutional Philosophy

The Constitution rests on several fundamental principles:

- Authority originates with a Principal.
- Delegated authority remains bounded.
- Accountability cannot be delegated.
- Verification is independent of execution.
- Evidence precedes trust.
- Halting unsafe work is always preferable to concealing uncertainty.
- Emergencies narrow authority rather than expand it.
- Constitutional authority cannot emerge through custom or repetition.

---

## Interpretation

The Constitution should be interpreted according to its ordinary meaning.

Defined constitutional terms carry the meanings assigned in Article II.

Lowercase or inflected forms of defined terms carry the same meaning where context clearly requires.

No interpretation should create authority that the Constitution does not expressly grant.

---

## Commentary Maintenance

This Commentary may evolve independently of the Constitution provided it does not alter constitutional meaning.

Changes to Commentary are not constitutional amendments.
