# Amendment A-0001 — Halt on Insufficient or Ambiguous Authority

## Status

Ratified.

## Constitutional Deficiency

Article VII, Section 4 requires an Executor to Halt when available authority is insufficient or ambiguous. Article IX, Section 2 otherwise describes its listed grounds as exhaustive for Executor- or Evaluator-initiated Halts, except as provided in Article X.

The two provisions can be reconciled through Boundary protection, but the relationship is not explicit enough for a constitutional baseline.

## Amendment

Article VII, Section 4 is amended to read:

> When available authority is insufficient or ambiguous, the Executor shall Halt under Article IX because continuing would exceed or risk exceeding its Boundary.

No other constitutional text is changed.

## Effect

This amendment clarifies that an Executor Halt caused by insufficient or ambiguous authority is an Article IX Halt grounded in actual or threatened Boundary exceedance.

It does not create a new Halt ground, expand Executor authority, narrow Principal authority, or alter Emergency authority.
