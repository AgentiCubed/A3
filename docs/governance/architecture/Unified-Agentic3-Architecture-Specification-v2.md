# Unified Agentic³ Architecture Specification v2

- **Status:** Canonical integration skeleton v2 (draft v0.1)
- **Purpose:** Integrate approved and proposed Agentic³ architecture artifacts
  without replacing their authority, evidence, or revision history

This skeleton is the canonical integration artifact for the architecture
workstream. Linked source artifacts remain authoritative for their own content.
Empty sections identify work still requiring its own governed review; they do
not imply approval or authority.

## 1. Executive Summary

Agentic³ is a governed engineering system that separates intent, execution,
verification, assurance, and authorization while preserving durable evidence
and human authority.

## 2. Constitutional Hierarchy

The [Constitution](../constitution/Constitution-v1.0.md) is supreme within its
jurisdiction. Subordinate architecture, governance, ontology, runtime, memory,
and implementation artifacts cannot create authority that the Constitution does
not grant.

## 3. Foundational Concepts (AC-0001, AC-0002)

Integrate the versioned Foundational Concepts artifacts after their governed
review, preserving candidate status and provenance.

## 4. Architectural Candidates (AC-0003–AC-0006)

Integrate Architectural Contracts, Separation of Decision and Execution,
Explicit Relationships, and Semantic Inheritance without treating supporting
evidence as automatic promotion.

## 5. Ontology

Integrate the canonical ontology vocabulary, entity taxonomy, relationship
semantics, identity rules, and versioning policy when available in this repository.

## 6. Executive, Intelligence, Assurance, and Runtime Domains

The [Runtime Domain Specification v1.0](../runtime/Runtime-Domain-Specification-v1.0.md)
defines continuous coordination and its authorization boundary. Other domain
contracts are incorporated here by durable link after approval.

## 7. Engine Architectural Contracts

Each engine contract states Mission, Inputs, Outputs, Dependencies, Authority,
Constraints, Evidence, and Learning. Cross-engine APIs remain logical contracts
until a governed technology decision is made.

## 8. Knowledge & Memory Architecture

The [Institutional Knowledge System](../knowledge/README.md) distinguishes fact,
interpretation, idea evolution, and reasoning. Runtime may propose capture but
cannot promote its own records to institutional truth.

## 9. Governance Model

Governance grants, denies, narrows, halts, or escalates bounded action. It does
not manufacture verification evidence or infer approval from silence.

## 10. Verification & Assurance Model

Execution, Verification, Assurance, and acceptance remain distinct. Verification
evaluates evidence; Assurance evaluates readiness and unresolved risk;
Governance supplies authority.

## 11. Knowledge Gravity Review Protocol

Gravity changes require evidence-backed reasons, explicit relationships,
review triggers, and governed promotion when higher architectural layers are
affected.

## 12. Engineering Genome Lifecycle

Genome membership requires demonstrated use, explicit adoption, versioning,
evidence, and reversal conditions. Runtime observations are signals, not
authority.

## 13. Architectural Candidate Register

The register records each candidate's status, supporting and contradictory
evidence, authority, dependencies, review trigger, and supersession history.

## 14. TARP-0001 Traceability

Material decisions must remain traceable from Constitution through concepts,
ontology, candidates, contracts, work, implementation, Verification,
Assurance/Governance, Memory, and Learning.

## 15. Implementation Roadmap

Implementation proceeds in separately authorized, reversible slices. Technology
selection must preserve canonical identity, provenance, idempotency, state
history, and authorization boundaries.

## 16. Glossary & Entity Taxonomy

The glossary references canonical definitions rather than duplicating them.
Conflicts or missing terms are recorded for ontology review instead of silently
resolved here.
