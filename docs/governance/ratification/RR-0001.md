# RR-0001 — Ratification Record

## Record

Document: AgentiCubed Constitution v1.0

Ratification Status: Ratified

Effective Version: v1.0

## Review History

The Constitution underwent multiple drafting iterations, adversarial review, controlled refactoring, and independent mechanical audit.

The review process included:

- architectural review
- constitutional review
- adversarial review
- controlled integration
- mechanical audit
- vocabulary audit
- cross-reference audit
- contradiction audit
- delegation-flow audit
- emergency-authority audit
- implementation-leakage audit
- minimality audit

The final constitutional text satisfied all mandatory ratification gates.

## Ratification Decision

The Principal ratified Constitution v1.0 as the supreme governance document of the AgentiCubed governance system.

All future constitutional modifications shall proceed exclusively through the amendment process defined in Article XII.

## Constitutional Baseline

This record establishes Constitution v1.0 as the constitutional baseline from which all future amendments are measured.
