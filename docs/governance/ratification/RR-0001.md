# RR-0001 — Ratification Record

## Record

**Document:** AgentiCubed Constitution v1.0

**Ratification Status:** Ratified

**Effective Version:** v1.0 with Amendment A-0001

---

## Original Ratification

The AgentiCubed Constitution v1.0 was ratified following constitutional drafting, adversarial review, controlled refactoring, and independent mechanical audit.

At the time of ratification, the Constitution was accepted as the supreme governance document of the AgentiCubed project.

---

## Constitutional History

Following ratification, a post-ratification review identified an ambiguity between Article VII, Section 4 and Article IX, Section 2 concerning Executor Halts arising from insufficient or ambiguous authority.

The first constitutional amendment is recorded separately as:

- Amendment A-0001 — Halt on Insufficient or Ambiguous Authority

Amendment A-0001 was ratified under Article XII and is effective as a clarification of Article VII, Section 4.

---

## Current Constitutional Authority

The currently effective constitutional authority consists of:

- AgentiCubed Constitution v1.0
- Ratified Amendment A-0001

Constitution v1.0 remains the original constitutional baseline and, together with Ratified Amendment A-0001, constitutes the current constitutional authority.

---

## Record Maintenance

This ratification record preserves the historical fact of the original v1.0 ratification and records subsequent constitutional amendments.

Future adopted amendments shall be appended to this record without altering the historical record of prior ratifications.
