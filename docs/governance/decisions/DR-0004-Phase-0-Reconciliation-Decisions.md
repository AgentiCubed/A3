# DR-0004 — Phase 0 Reconciliation Decisions

## Purpose

Record the Principal's rulings on the decision queue P-1 through P-7 raised
by the Phase 0 Reconciliation Record
(`docs/agentic3/PHASE0-RECONCILIATION.md` §2 and §5), so that each ruling is
a durable governance decision rather than a conversational fact.

## Context

The Phase 0 reconciliation of the Agentic³ source families surfaced seven
questions reserved to the Principal, each with an Executor recommendation.
On 2026-07-26 the Principal (James Richmond) explicitly accepted all seven
recommendations. Per DR-0001's principle that agreement among Executors does
not constitute ratification, the operative act is the Principal's acceptance;
this record preserves it. The same acceptance resolved the recorded status
dispute over AC-0001/AC-0002 (four documents said Adopted; the concept
documents said Proposed with no adoption record — and two of AC-0001's
reopening conditions were met and unactioned).

## Decision

1. **P-1 — AC-0001 Knowledge Gravity is Adopted, as amended.** The
   ontology representation (ONTO-0004) is amended to conform to the
   concept: gravity changes flow only through the four bounded sources
   (Evidence, Reuse, Contradiction, Supersession) — review, demotion, and
   promotion are recorded as governed *outcomes* of events from those four
   sources, not as source event types — and the "gravity is authority"
   wording is struck throughout: gravity is justified weight and never
   outranks a Delegation, an acceptance condition, or a Principal decision.
   Both met reopening conditions are hereby actioned and closed by these
   amendments.
2. **P-2 — AC-0002 Engineering Genome is Adopted, as amended.** ONTO-0004
   §2.2's eight element kinds are the adopted *representation*; the
   ARCHITECTURE-v2 §7 candidate lifecycle is the adopted *entry procedure*
   for genome membership.
3. **P-3 — GD-P1 is decided: yes.** The ARCHITECTURE-v2 §7 candidate
   lifecycle governs layer-2 Foundational Concepts and Engineering Genome
   membership exactly as it governs layer-4 candidates. One lifecycle, all
   candidate layers.
4. **P-4 — Review instruments.** STD-0002 remains the *Active standard*;
   TARP-0001 is **Adopted** as the *protocol* STD-0002 implements. Citations
   shall use each instrument's real name.
5. **P-5 — The Verification/Assurance boundary is adopted:** *Verification
   evaluates evidence; Assurance evaluates readiness and unresolved risk;
   Governance supplies authority.* ARCHITECTURE-v2 risk R3 is closed.
6. **P-6 — AC-0003…AC-0006 statuses are unchanged.** The four missing
   concept documents shall be commissioned and land (lifecycle state 1)
   before any promotion review.
7. **P-7 — "Independence" is defined** for every promotion gate that
   requires independent workstreams: a workstream is independent of another
   when it has a different initiating issue, a different primary Executor
   session, and no shared draft lineage.

## Consequences

### Positive

- The Foundational-Concept layer is no longer self-contradictory: adoption
  is now an explicit, recorded governance act, and every status register
  agrees with the concept documents.
- Phase 0's acceptance line "all candidate/adopted statuses are explicit"
  is satisfiable; Phase 1 has a stable layer to build on.

### Costs

- The ONTO-0004 amendments (P-1) narrow a shipped draft; any future need
  for additional gravity-event sources requires a governed revision of
  AC-0001, not an ontology edit.
- The P-7 independence definition is deliberately strict; some evidence
  that feels independent will not count. That friction is the gate working.

## Authority and scope

This Decision Record is subordinate to the AgentiCubed Constitution. Its
authority is the Principal's explicit acceptance of 2026-07-26, recorded in
RR-0002 alongside the ratification of DR-0003. It applies to the Agentic³
architecture, ontology, and governance documents in this repository. Where
this document conflicts with the Constitution, the Constitution controls.
