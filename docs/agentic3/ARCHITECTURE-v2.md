## 1. Purpose

Agentic³ is an engineering intelligence system that governs, coordinates, verifies, remembers, and continuously improves work performed by humans, AI agents, tools, and automation across time.

It is not a single model, chatbot, or vendor integration. Models and tools are replaceable participants.
